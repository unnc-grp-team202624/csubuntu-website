IndustrialSense: Software Platform for an Optical Sensing and Process Monitoring Prototype

Group Research Project (COMP2043) · School of Computer Science · University of Nottingham Ningbo China · 2026–27

Project

IndustrialSense is a software engineering project around an industrial engineering prototype that combines optical sensing components, supporting sensors, control hardware, and a supervisory computer. Our team will run a full software engineering cycle, from requirements engineering with the industrial stakeholders to the design, implementation, and verification of a platform that acquires, processes, stores, visualises, and helps interpret process data.
